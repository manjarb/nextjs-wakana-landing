# WANAKA Sanctuary – Brand Summary

## 🧘‍♀️ Overview
**WANAKA Sanctuary** presents itself as *“Everyday Sanctuary — Small Rituals. Big Rest”*, a wellness and spa brand designed to offer moments of calm and rejuvenation in daily life. It aims to balance luxury and accessibility, particularly for busy women and families seeking mental and physical rest.

---

## 🌿 Brand Concept
- **Big Idea:** *Everyday Sanctuary* — small self-care rituals that lead to deep relaxation.  
- **Winning Point:** Positions itself as *“the calm between drop-off and pick-up”* — a luxury that fits real life.  
- **Core Pillars (3Cs):**
  - Calm Experience  
  - Caring Hands  
  - Clean Craft  

---

## 💭 About & Vision
**About Us:**  
WANAKA believes happiness and confidence stem from a balanced body and mind. It’s a place for rest, pampering, and family bonding — “a sanctuary for your skin, body, and mind.”

**Vision:**  
To become the most trusted facial and nail spa in Bangna district — a sanctuary for moms, families, and professionals.  
**Targets:**  
- 500 members in the first year  
- 4.7+ customer rating within 18 months

---

## 🎯 Mission
WANAKA promises to *“craft moments that feel wonderful.”*  
- All services are performed by trained therapists to the highest standards.  
- Products are safe, natural, and high-quality — rooted in nature.

## 👩‍👧 Target Guests

### 1. **Primary Persona – “School-Run Sanctuary Mom”**
- **Profile:** Thai/Asian or expat women (32–48), income THB 150k–400k/month, based in Bangna–Srinakarin.  
- **Behavior:** Visits between 9:30–14:30 (weekdays), once a week for “ME time.”  
- **Mindset:** Seeks clean, gentle, precise service and warmth.  
- **Pain Points:** Limited time, parking hassles, skin sensitivity, dislikes upselling.  
- **Budget:** THB 1,500–3,000/visit (2–4 times/month).  
- **Needs:** Quick body & mind reset, visible freshness and glow, safety for pregnancy and kids.

### 2. **Secondary Persona – “After Work Modern”**
- **Profile:** Female professionals or entrepreneurs (28–38), minimalist design lovers.  
- **Context:** Visits evenings (18:00–20:00) or weekends.  
- **Lifestyle:** Values emotional experience, work-life balance, calm post-work decompression.  
- **Pain Points:** Poor technique, redness, pushy sales.  
- **Budget:** THB 2,000–3,500/visit (1–2 times/month).  
- **Needs:** Brightening, anti-aging, quiet reflection time.

## ✨ Experience Design
WANAKA emphasizes a *“multi-sensory and immersive journey”* across all touchpoints:

### **Multi-Sensory Journey**
- **Sound:** Custom ambient soundscapes per room (e.g., water sounds, healing tones).  
- **Aroma:** “Aroma Zoning” — scents differ by treatment (lavender, citrus, eucalyptus).  
- **Lighting:** Adjustable warm and cool lighting for relaxation or energy.

### **Immersive Journey**
- **Welcome Ritual:** Guests pick a “Mood Card” (Relax / Glow / Recharge) — matched with a themed welcome drink (e.g., pomegranate, pandan, honey-lemon).  
- **Bath Bomb Ritual:** Guests select a scent-matched foot soak.  
- **Wall Visuals:** Nature projections or ambient animations.  
- **Sound Bath Element:** 2–3 minute sound therapy before or after treatments.  
- **Surprise Gifts:** Random wellness treats or gemstone bracelets for positivity.  
- **Collecting Journey:** Loyalty system with stamps or points redeemable for rewards.

## 🪞 Summary
**WANAKA Sanctuary** is a modern, family-inclusive spa that combines mindfulness, sensory immersion, and hospitality. It offers “luxury that fits real life,” encouraging guests to embrace small self-care rituals for big rest.
